# L3 LG37: last-use moves of str_from_utf8 and resize on LLVM, loop head scopes in one walk

Scope: G-DB56-4 (`str_from_utf8(b)` of an owned local `[u8]` at its last use copies the block on LLVM), G-DB49-2
(`resize` of an owned local array at its last use copies into a new block on LLVM), G-LG31-6 (pruvc-rs
`frame_candidates` rebuilds the head scope of each pushing loop with a fresh walk of the function). Base: main
4ccd03a0. No bench source and no older L3 doc was edited.

Status: LG37: G-DB56-4 closed (on both compilers `str_from_utf8(b)` of an owned local or take parameter at its last use takes b's block; a later read keeps the copy)
Status: LG37: G-DB49-2 closed (on both compilers `resize(x, n, v)` of an owned local at its last use grows x's block in place through `@pruv_regrow*`; a later read keeps `@pruv_resize*`)
Status: LG37: G-LG31-6 closed (pruvc-rs `frame_candidates` builds every loop head scope in one walk, `loop_envs`; verifier traces identical old vs new on 2858 of 2862 inputs, the 4 others unstable on the old binary too)

## Step 1: str_from_utf8 takes the block at its last use (G-DB56-4)

The LLVM generator (`pruvc/llvmgen.py`, `pruvc-rs/crates/pruv-core/src/llvmgen.rs`) already moved a whole `[u8]`
local into a `str` at `let s: str = str_from_utf8(b)`. A `str_from_utf8(b)` nested in a return value, a call
argument or any other operand still went through `@pruv_copy_v2`. `nested_moves(e, target, ret)` now collects the
`[u8]` / array locals whose only remaining use is one operand of `str_from_utf8` or `resize` inside `e` (the slot is
a local, or a take parameter the function owns at a return; not a borrowed or inout parameter, a reference, a pinned
or a stack local), and the statement lowering sets them as `moving` while it evaluates the value. The lowering of
`str_from_utf8(b)` with `b` moving hands b's pointer, length and capacity to the `str` and does not free b.

## Step 2: resize grows the block in place at its last use (G-DB49-2)

`resize(x, n, v)` with `x` moving calls `regrow_block` (split out of `regrow_val`), which reuses the
`@pruv_regrow` / `@pruv_regrow_u8` / `@pruv_regrow_narrow` helpers of `x = resize(x, n, v)`: the block is
reallocated to `select(n > cap, n, cap)`, the tail is filled with `v` (zero fill for `u8` 0 / `bool` false through
the same helper), and x is not freed. A later read of `x` keeps the copying `@pruv_resize*`.

The special statement paths that evaluate operands outside the generic `let` / assignment lowering also set the
moving set (D-LG37-2): the fill of a stack `let`, the operands of an in-place `x = resize(x, ...)` and of a field
regrow, both operands of an index assignment (counted together), and the length of a `return truncate(...)`.

## Step 3: loop head scopes in one walk (G-LG31-6)

`loop_envs(f)` (`pruvc-rs/crates/pruv-proof/src/infer.rs`) walks the function once with the rules of
`find_loop_env` and records the environment at the head of every `while`, keyed by node address.
`frame_candidates` builds it on the first loop that pushes and falls back to `scope_types(f, w)` for a missing key.

Tests: `tests/runtime/lg37_rt_move` (functions `retstr`, `argstr`, `keeparg` for step 1; `grow`, `zbytes`, `within`,
`flags`, `keepsrc` for step 2 grow, `u8` zero fill, shrink within capacity, `bool`, aliasing source kept; `asg`,
`rtr`, `stk`, `idx`, `su8` for the special statement paths), with `llvm_fn_contains` / `llvm_fn_absent` on the
helpers and `alloc_balance`.
