Brief: close or move D-DB25-1 (window time), D-DB25-2 (block_arrays window RSS) and D-DB25-3 (head/tail RSS).
Byte-identical output, no compiler edits, lane `bench/l3/claude/` and `bench/l3/lib/{access,parse,json}.pruv`.
Survey: docs/L3_DB25.md. Base: main 5272ab88.

## What landed

- `bench/l3/lib/access.pruv`: `pub fn fields_into(doc, id, n, k0..k7: str, inout out: [i64]) -> () ! alloc`,
  a last-wins lookup of the first `n` keys of object `id` in one walk of its members. Each `out[s]` equals
  `field(doc, id, ks)` (same -1/-2/-3/-4 codes; slots at or past `n` get -1); keys that are not ASCII or longer
  than 16 MiB fall back to `field` per slot. Helpers `hit` (the `find_ascii` prefilter plus `jstr::ascii_equal`),
  `slot_error`, `fill_error`, `field_if_used`, `last_match`. `field` and every old API are unchanged.
- `bench/l3/claude/entry.pruv`: `root_of(doc) -> [i64]` reads the seven root members (`type`, `timestamp`,
  `isMeta`, `isSidechain`, `message`, `isApiErrorMessage`, `origin`) in one pass; `block_of` reads `type`/`text`
  in one pass; `user_text_of(doc, r)` takes the root slots. `user_text(doc)` keeps its old lazy body.
  `string_of`, `object_of`, `true_of`, `string_field_of` turn a raw slot into the old per-field results with
  explicit range checks.
- `bench/l3/claude/import.pruv`: `read_window` calls `root_of` once per line and passes the slots to
  `consume_entry_of`, which also reads `message.model`/`message.content` in one pass. `consume_entry` keeps its
  old contract as a wrapper.
- The lookup returns slots through an inout `[i64]`, not a struct: with a `Members`/`Root` struct the rs verifier
  timed out on the unrelated `turns::plan_end` postcondition (G-DB29-1).
- `bench/l3/claude/turns.pruv`: one `assert data[from + j] == old(data)[from + j];` before the copy in
  `move_stamp`. With `fields_into` in the crate the py verifier timed out on that loop invariant (D-DB29-2); the
  assert gives it the read-before-write fact and py verifies `a4_root.pruv` in 52 s.
- Not done: tape-shape ensures on `parse::scan` to drop `check_tape`, and scanning the borrowed line without a
  copy (D-DB29-1, G-DB25-2). `lib/parse.pruv` and `lib/json.pruv` are unchanged.
