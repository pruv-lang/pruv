## What changed (step 1)

`bench/l3/lib/ent_value.pruv` gets two blocks.

- "DB17: entry slot helpers" (before the DB14 header):
  - `pstr_off`, `pstr_raw_len`, `pstr_set_off`, `pstr_put_prefix`.
  - `pool_copy_into` (range copy into a preallocated output, returns the end), `pool_copy_prefix`,
    `pool_copy_range`.
  - `pool_eq`, `pool_hash`, `packet_pool_ok`.
  - `logical_pool_words` (moved from `entry_api`) and `put_title_prefix` (moved from `ent_dispatch`), both `pub`.
- "DB17: req slot helpers" (end of file):
  - `pool_copy_all` (whole pool into a preallocated packet).
  - `pool_clamp_base`, `pool_prefix`.
  - `packed_pool_write_size`, `packed_pool_words`, `packed_pool_ends_at`, `packed_pool_read`, `packed_pool_copy`,
    `packed_pool_put`.
  - `pslot_empty`, `pslot_is_empty`, `pslot_host_words`, `pslot_copy`, `pslot_copy_off`, `pslot_copy_off_clamped`,
    `pslots_rebase`, `pslot_needs_clip`, `pslot_ok`.

Callers:

- entry: `ent_bound` (prefix slot, key compare, preallocated output), `entry_api` compact await, `ent_packet` and
  `ent_state` (logical pool size vs physical section/buffer length now named apart), `ent_dispatch`.
- req: `req_child` in1..in4, `req_list` (scmp, pack, packed pool), `req_sources` empty slot, `req_value`
  (`str_ok`, `needs_clip`, `rebased_off` and the rebase body removed; `slot_ok` sends tag 6 to `pslot_ok`),
  `root.pruv` pool merges including the old `:811` line.
- `req/q_mutants.py`:
  - M14's text pattern now matches `crate::ent_value::pool_cat(p, cpool)`, the new form of the old `root.pruv:811`
    line. The brief placed M14 in `entry/q_mutants.py`; it is in `req/q_mutants.py`.
  - `build_host_copy` now reuses `host/src/lib.rs` with its includes replaced, instead of a separate copy. This is
    outside the asked sites; it was needed for the mutant build after the req host include list moved.

The two workers each added a `pool_copy_into` with a different signature. The req one was renamed to
`pool_copy_all` at merge, and its 4 `req_child` callers were updated.
