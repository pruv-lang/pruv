# L3 DB41: supervisor reads into reused buffers (D-R10-3/4, D-DB9-1..4)

Brief: move the supervisor event loop to `ev_read_into` / `ev_proc_read_into` (LG7) with one reused buffer per
stream, then re-measure whole-lane time and RSS and the core grid (old = main c068c108, hand Rust = 1.0x). No
compiler, runtime, `bench/l3/lib` or `bench/l3/pkgs` edits. The rs compiler was built once from this base
(`pruvc-rs/target/release/pruvc`, 01:15 KST).

## What landed

- `bench/l3/supervisor/supervise.pruv`: the 4 `ev_proc_read` calls and the 1 `ev_read` call (each followed by
  `ev_size` + `ev_bytes`, which copied the read into a fresh array) are now `ev_proc_read_into` / `ev_read_into`.
  - One buffer per stream, allocated once before the loop: `b1`, `b2` (65536 bytes, the old per-call maximum of the
    process reader) and `bs` (4096 bytes, the old socket maximum).
  - `take` gets `subview(b, 0, n)`, a borrowed slice of the bytes read, so no per-read array is built.
  - The stop/drain loop counts `got` from the same `n` values. Stop detection on the socket is `ns > 0`, which was
    `ev_size(l) > 0` before.
  - `take`, `capfile.pruv`, `btail.pruv` and the harness are unchanged.
- `measure_r10.py` is unchanged. The measurements below used a scratch driver that runs old, new and hand in rotating
  order on the same inputs.

## Measurements

10 alternating rounds per cell, medians, same machine. Load average (1 min) was 7.0 -> 4.8 during the whole-lane
runs and 3.6 -> 3.9 during the core runs. Ratios are against hand.

### Whole lane (chunk 65536, T 4096)

| cell | hand time | old time | new time | hand RSS | old RSS | new RSS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| rs/rust M | 84.4 ms | 0.973x | 0.968x | 19 382 272 | 1.053x | 1.043x |
| rs/rust L | 320.7 ms | 0.961x | 0.944x | 69 713 920 | 1.015x | 1.012x |
| rs/llvm M | 85.6 ms | 0.955x | 0.939x | 19 382 272 | 1.059x | 1.048x |
| rs/llvm L | 315.8 ms | 0.956x | 0.947x | 69 713 920 | 1.017x | 1.014x |

Time is under hand on every cell, and was already under hand on old. The DB9 numbers (rs/rust L 1.116x, rs/llvm
1.076-1.154x) came before LG7's runtime read path. The `_into` move gains another 0.5-1.7%.

RSS split. The L - M slope is 1.000 for old, new and hand, which is the input-sized capture the harness checks, so the
proportional share is 0. The fixed share (RSS minus input size) at rs/rust is 2.605 MB hand, 3.637 MB old, 3.441 MB new.
Small inputs show where it comes from (bytes; RSS minus input size):

| input | hand | rs/rust old | rs/rust new | rs/llvm old | rs/llvm new |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 64 KiB | 2 490 368 | 2 572 288 | 2 572 288 | 2 654 208 | 2 654 208 |
| 1 MiB | 2 490 368 | 2 646 016 | 2 588 672 | 2 711 552 | 2 686 976 |
| 4 MiB | 2 523 136 | 3 637 248 | 3 440 640 | 3 735 552 | 3 555 328 |

- The base excess (+82 KB rust, +164 KB llvm) is binary and runtime size: hand 699 400 bytes, rs/rust 733 480,
  rs/llvm 737 976.
- The step of about +0.9 MB once the input is over 1 MiB is the runtime's process read-ahead. It queues up to 1 MiB
  per stream before it blocks. The lane cannot set that bound (G-DB41-1). Hand uses a sync channel of 2 x 64 KiB.
- The `_into` move removed the per-read arrays (about -0.2 MB at 4 MiB).

|---|---|---|---|---|

| M c4/T65536 | 0.949x | 1.011x | 1.014x | 1.012x |

| L c4/T4096 | 0.949x | 1.001x | 1.011x | 1.001x |
| L c4/T65536 | 0.950x | 1.003x | 1.026x | 1.003x |

The core RSS excess is fixed: +65 KB at T4096 and +213 KB at T65536, the same at M and L. It grows with T, not with
input. rs/llvm time is 0.8-2.6% over hand. Neither has a cause that is small and inside the lane without a profile, so
both are recorded here (the brief says to stop at the next item that needs a new analysis).
