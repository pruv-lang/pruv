## 3. Why steps 2 and 3 stopped

- The ord and dense rows are the grok history reader (`read::window`). `run_perf.py` compiles the grok modules util,
  packet, path, framing, entry, read with a manifest that links json, parse, access, jstr, lines, bytes, text, time,
  names, turns from `bench/l3/pkgs/`, not from `bench/l3/lib/`.
- The parts named in D-DB15-1 (escape count pass and per-string allocation in `jstr`/`access`, host `from_utf8` in
  `utf8_to_string`) and D-DB15-2 (`parse::scan_into` record writes, `framing::find_lf`, `access::view_root_type`) are in
  `bench/l3/pkgs/{jstr,access,parse}` and `bench/l3/grok/{framing,read,entry,util}.pruv`. Both are outside this lane.
- `bench/l3/lib` does not even hold the DB21 work: `lib/access.pruv`, `jstr.pruv`, `parse.pruv` and `lines.pruv` differ
  from their pkgs copies (no plain-string mark, no `string_text_plain`). A change there would not reach the gate rows.
- G-DB15-1 (host `from_utf8`) is a compiler/verifier gap (LG lane).
