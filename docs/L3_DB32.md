# L3 DB32: codex reader RSS, the per-turn copies and the second wire text (D-DB26-1, D-DB28-1, D-DB28-3)

Brief: shrink or close D-DB26-1 (codex RSS) and D-DB28-1 (per-turn `pbytes` / `fbuf` copies in `replay`, second
wire text next to the `finish2` result), and measure D-DB28-3 (rs/llvm not remeasured) (docs/L3_RESTRUCTURE_SPEC.md
7.5). Byte-identical output, no compiler edits, lane `bench/l3/codex/` and add-only `bench/l3/pkgs/turns/turns.pruv`.
Base: main 2a79f491.

## What landed

- Step 1, second wire text (`read.pruv`, `packet.pruv`): `replay` sizes the wire from `turns::text_layout` (header
  of 20 + 10 * kept + 2 * items words, then `base` bytes, then the turn texts) and writes the selected texts straight
  into it with `turns::finish_into` (as qwen does since DB31). `packet::read_header` validates the read shape and
  writes the header words into the already sized wire; it fails exactly where `wire_read` failed. The `finish2` text
  and its copy into the wire are gone.
- Step 2, per-turn copies (`read.pruv`, `pkgs/turns/turns.pruv` add-only): `feed` takes byte spans of `hb`
  (`po, pn` prompt, `ro, rn` reply, `pad` 0 or 4 for the U+10000 tail) instead of `str`s. New in `turns`:
  `begin3` / `answer3` (`begin2` / `answer2` over `src[lo..lo+n)`), and the private helpers `clip_span` (longest
  prefix at most `max` that ends before a non-continuation byte, the `clip_bytes` cut) and `write_span` (separator,
  span, pad bytes into the store). `pbytes`, `fbuf`, the `pad` join and the two `adapter_probe` decodes are gone.
  `answer3` requires `n + pad <= 100000000` (the `len(text)` bound of `answer2`). A span that is not valid UTF-8 counts as empty, as the decode did; `replay` checks that with `span_ok`
  (`utf8_valid(subview(hb, ...))`), because `[u8]` carries no UTF-8 fact (G-DB32-1).
- Step 3, `hb` lifetime (`read.pruv`, `root.pruv`): `finish` / `replay` take `hb` by `take` / `move` and drop it
  (`hb = alloc(0, 0)`) after the feed loop, before `text_layout` and the wire allocation, so the held text, the store
  and the wire are not live together. `hb` and `line` still grow by doubling (D-DB32-1).
